# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

HealthSync Uganda exists to serve patients, clinicians and public servants
across the country. Everyone who interacts with this project — contributors,
maintainers, users — deserves a harassment-free experience, regardless of age,
ability, ethnicity, sex, gender identity, religion, language, region of origin,
clinical role, or political affiliation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour we expect from contributors:

- Treat colleagues, citizens and patients with dignity.
- Assume positive intent. Ask before assuming.
- Defer to clinical staff on clinical safety questions.
- Acknowledge and credit prior work, including community translations and
  field reports.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.

Examples of behaviour that will not be tolerated:

- Personal attacks, insults, slurs, or harassment of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery; unwelcome sexual attention.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Sharing or requesting **real patient data** in any forum tied to this
  project (issues, PRs, chat, demos). All test data must be synthetic.
- Discrimination on the basis of HIV status, disability, mental health,
  pregnancy, or any other protected attribute.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — GitHub repositories, mailing lists,
chat channels, conferences, and any venue where someone is representing the
project — as well as 1:1 interactions when the contributor is identifiable as
representing HealthSync Uganda.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behaviour can be sent in confidence to
**conduct@healthsync.ug**. All reports are reviewed within five working days.

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

1. A private written warning.
2. A temporary ban from project spaces.
3. A permanent ban from the project.
4. For PHI-related violations: notification of the Personal Data Protection
   Office (PDPO) under the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019.

Maintainers who fail to enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the
project's governance team.
